---
name: Clear copy
kind: copy
---

## 01 Lead with what the reader gets
Open a page, section or paragraph with the outcome or the answer. History, team and method come after, if at all.
- Before: Founded in 2014, our team has spent years building a platform for modern kitchens.
- After: Plan a month of meals in ten minutes.

## 02 Be specific
Swap vague words ("solutions", "innovative", "seamless", "world-class") for what the thing does, a number or an example. If there is nothing specific to say, cut the sentence.
- Before: Our innovative solution streamlines your workflow.
- After: Invoices are sent the day a job closes, not at the end of the month.

## 03 Active voice, with someone doing something
Say who does what. Passive sentences and fragments hide the actor and read like notes.
- Before: Orders are processed within 24 hours.
- After: We ship every order within 24 hours.

## 04 One idea per sentence
Split a sentence that carries two or three ideas. Long lists inside a sentence usually want to be two sentences.
- Before: Sign up today to get early access, free shipping on your first order, and a chance to shape what we build next.
- After: Sign up for early access and free shipping on your first order. Early members help decide what we build next.

## 05 The reader's words, not yours
Use the words customers use. Internal names, project code names and jargon need a plain version or an explanation.
- Before: Manage your SKUs in the PIM.
- After: Edit your product list in one place.

## 06 Cut filler
Remove words that add length but no meaning: "in order to", "simply", "very", "actually", "it's important to note that".
- Before: In order to get started, simply create an account.
- After: Create an account to start.

## 07 Claims you can back up
Superlatives and promises ("the best", "guaranteed", "never fails") need proof on the page or a softer, true version. Check numbers and names against the source.
- Before: The fastest checkout on the web.
- After: Checkout takes three taps.

## 08 Buttons say what happens
A button or link names the action and its result. Avoid "Click here", "Learn more" and "Submit" on their own.
- Before: Learn more
- After: See pricing

## 09 Numbers as digits
Write numbers as digits in running text when they carry information (prices, times, counts). Spell out "one" only in casual phrasing.
- Before: Delivery takes two to three days.
- After: Delivery takes 2 to 3 days.

## 10 Same thing, same word
Pick one name for each thing and use it everywhere. Switching between "account", "profile" and "workspace" makes readers think they are three things.
- Before: Open your workspace settings, then invite people to your account.
- After: Open your workspace settings, then invite people to your workspace.
